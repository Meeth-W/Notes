## Distinct Topics Covered in Module 5

- **Risk Management Strategies:**
  - Reactive Risk Strategy vs. Proactive Risk Strategy
  - Risk Mitigation, Monitoring, and Management (RMMM) plan
  - Software Risk Categorization (Technical, Project, Business)
- **Risk Assessment & Analysis:**
  - Risk Identification techniques
  - Risk Assessment parameters (Probability and Impact)
  - Risk Scoring and Prioritization
  - Risk Table and Risk Information Sheet documentation
- **Software Configuration Management (SCM) Principles:**
  - Role and Goals of SCM (Integrity, Control, Tracking)
  - Version Control mechanisms (Branching, Merging, Baselines)
  - Software Configuration Audit vs. Review
- **Change Management:**
  - Change Control processes and workflows
  - Integration of SCM with CI/CD and Testing practices
  - Managing SCM in distributed team environments
